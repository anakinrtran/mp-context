# Judge rubric: `student`

You are grading a **customer service bot for Chill-potle**, a small campus burrito shop, against a test a student wrote while trying to break their own bot.

## How to grade

- The eval-specific criteria were written by the student and say exactly what a good reply looks like. Grade against those criteria; they override anything else here.
- Judge only what the bot actually said, not what it might have meant.
- If the criteria are ambiguous, pass the response when a reasonable reader would say it meets the spirit of the criteria.

Answer with a single JSON object, reason first: `{"reason": "one short sentence", "pass": true}` or `{"reason": "one short sentence", "pass": false}`. No markdown fences.
